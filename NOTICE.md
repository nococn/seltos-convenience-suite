# Notices & Open Source Attributions

This project integrates, adapts, and builds upon several pioneering open-source projects in the Hyundai-Kia Gen5W automotive infotainment community. We express our deepest gratitude to the original authors and maintainers:

1. **HMG-API & HMG-Mod**
   - Authors: `xwtk`, `VysseCodes`
   - Purpose: Framework for DiPO CAN cluster navigation rendering and iAP2 turn-by-turn packet handling on Gen5W head units.
   - License: GNU AGPL-3.0

2. **NEMESIS Automotive Mod Manager**
   - Author: `seogaemo`
   - Purpose: Package installer format (`NEMESIS.data`), service manager, and lifecycle hooks for Gen5W infotainment.
   - Link: https://seogaemo.github.io

3. **Korea Projection**
   - Maintainer: `StopDragon`
   - Purpose: Diagnostic telemetry and Gen5W wireless research.
   - Link: https://github.com/StopDragon/korea-projection

4. **Leafia AAC Audio Mod**
   - Purpose: High-resolution AAC audio codec decoding engine replacing low-bitrate defaults.

---
**Seltos SP2PE Convenience Suite Integration & Daemons**
- Maintained by: Kim Do-hyeong ([@nococn](https://github.com/nococn))
- Purpose: Tailored solution for Kia Seltos SP2PE and unpopulated Wi-Fi (DNP) Gen5W head units, providing wired/dongle CarPlay cluster TBT, reverse-camera aware auto-launch, and AAC audio enhancement with A/B comparison toggle.
- License: GNU AGPL-3.0
